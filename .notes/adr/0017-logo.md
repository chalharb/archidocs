# 0017. Logo

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15

## Context and problem statement

A mark is needed for the header, favicon and package page.

## Decision

Option C, "Doorway tile".

The logo is the Doorway tile: a rounded square with an arched doorway and a violet keystone above the arch. It is a light tile on dark backgrounds and a dark tile on light ones, and must stay legible at 16 px.

## Consequences

- Good: distinctive at small sizes.
- Bad: assets are not in the repository yet; see the design notes.
