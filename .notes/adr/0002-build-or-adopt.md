# 0002. Build or adopt

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15

## Context and problem statement

Structurizr, LikeC4 and Archify (tt-a1i/archify) each have gaps that matter here: their own formats, no per-element change history, no decision links to many elements, or no model behind the diagram.

## Decision

Build.

Archidocs is built rather than adopted or forked.

## Consequences

- Good: free to fit CALM, decisions and history together.
- Bad: one developer carries the whole viewer, and there is no ecosystem.
