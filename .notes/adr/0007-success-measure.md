# 0007. Success measure

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15

## Context and problem statement

With no outside team yet, time-based targets would be invented numbers.

## Decision

Easy to consume and easy to maintain.

Success means readers answer their questions without opening source files and authors change the architecture by editing model files only. No time limits are set.

## Consequences

- Good: criteria that can be checked against the reference model.
- Bad: nothing forces a schedule.
