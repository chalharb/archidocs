Find any [Element](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-element), a Person, System, Service or Component of the Model, and see everything a [Team](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-team) owns.

## Before you start

- You have a viewer open: the [Big Bank demo ↗](https://big-bank.archidocs.dev), or your own Workspace with `archidocs dev`. See the [Quick start](/docs/start/quick-start/).

## 1. Search for the Element

Press `/`, or select **Find** in the rail on the left. Type part of a name, an id, a Team, a technology or one of a Service's URLs. Select a result to open it on the diagram.

To browse instead, use the other trees in the rail:

- **Model** lists every Element, by kind or by Team.
- **Views** lists the Generated and Saved Views. A [View](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-view) is a diagram drawn from a query over the Model.

To go back to where you were, use the back and forward buttons in the location bar, or its **Recent** menu.

## 2. Read its Element panel

Selecting a node opens the Element panel beside the diagram:

<Screenshot id="viewer-element-panel" alt="The Payments System's View with the Payments Service selected. The Element panel shows its Owner, the Payments Team, and buttons for Upstream & downstream, Connect to…, Open its View and Where is it deployed?" />

**Overview** shows the description, the [Owner](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-owner) Team, and the questions you can ask about the Element. The other tabs list its URLs, its ADRs, its [Relationships](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-relationship) under **Links**, and the Revisions that changed it under **History**.

To see what is inside a System or Service without leaving the View, select the **+** on its box.

## 3. See what the Team owns

Select **Teams** in the rail, then a Team. Or select the Owner in the Element panel. The Team Lens outlines every Element the Team owns.

A [Lens](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-lens) highlights part of a View without changing what it shows. Clear it with the **×** on its chip.

<Screenshot id="viewer-team-lens" alt="The Service Landscape with the Team Lens on the Payments Team: the Payments System and the Card Network are outlined." />

## Check it worked

The location bar shows a **Team** chip with the Team's name, and the address ends in `team=` and the Team's id. On the demo, [what the Payments Team owns ↗](https://big-bank.archidocs.dev/#/view/c2?team=payments) is the Payments System and the Card Network.

## With an agent

Ask "What does the Payments Team own?". [`list_owned`](/docs/reference/mcp/read-tools/#list_owned) answers it. [`search_elements`](/docs/reference/mcp/read-tools/#search_elements) finds an Element by name, and [`get_element`](/docs/reference/mcp/read-tools/#get_element) returns what its Element panel shows.
