A [View](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-view) is a diagram defined by a query over the Model. It is never a source of truth: it selects Elements and draws them, and the Model decides what they are and how they connect. Change the Model and every View that shows the change redraws.

## Generated Views

A [Generated View](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-generated-view) exists for every scope without anyone writing it:

| View id | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `c1` | The System Landscape: every Person and System. |
| `c2` | The Service Landscape: every Service, grouped in its System. |
| `c2/<system>` | One System's Services, with what they connect to. |
| `c3/<service>` | One Service's Components, with what they connect to. |
| `deployment/<environment>` | Every Deployment in one Environment, on its Targets. |

They cover most questions. The viewer opens on `c2`.

## Saved Views

A [Saved View](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-saved-view) is a View written into a Project's files, under `views`, for a question the Generated Views don't answer at a glance. A structural Saved View has a `level` and selectors. Big Bank's Digital Banking Team saved one for the mainframe:

```yaml file="examples/big-bank/archidocs/digital/views.yaml" region="mainframe-outage"
```

<Diagram view="big-bank/digital:mainframe-outage" alt="Everything that depends on the Big Bank mainframe, directly or not, with the Impact Lens on." />

- `include` picks Elements: by id, with their children or descendants, `upstream` or `downstream` of one, by `team`, by `tag`, or on the paths between two (`from` and `to`). `exclude` drops some again.
- `level` (`c1`, `c2`, `c3` or `deployment`) collapses what was picked to that level, and `expand` and `collapse` open or close single Elements.

A Saved View has a Qualified id, such as `digital:mainframe-outage`, and the selectors keep working as the Model changes: a new Service that depends on the mainframe joins this View without anyone editing it. Every field is in the [Views reference](/docs/reference/model/views-yaml/).

## Lenses

A [Lens](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-lens) highlights a View without changing which nodes it shows:

- **Impact**: what is upstream, downstream, or both, of one Element, each node marked with how many hops away it is.
- **Team**: what one Team owns.
- **Path**: the paths between two Elements.

The viewer turns Lenses on and off; a Saved View can open with one, as `mainframe-outage` opens with Impact.

## Deployment Views

A [Deployment View](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-deployment-view) places Deployments on their Deployment Targets in one Environment. Each Environment has a Generated one, and a Saved View with `level: deployment` narrows it to some Elements or `targets`. See [Deployments](/docs/concepts/deployments/#deployment-views).

## Dynamic Views

A [Dynamic View](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-dynamic-view) shows one scenario as an ordered list of [Steps](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-step), drawn as a sequence chart:

<Diagram view="big-bank/payments:make-a-payment" alt="A Big Bank customer makes a payment: numbered Steps from the customer through the app and the Payments Service to the mainframe, with the payment events and the reply." />

Each Step must follow a Relationship that exists in the Model, so a scenario can't show a call the architecture doesn't have. See [Dynamic Views](/docs/guides/model/dynamic-views/).

## Layout

The [Layout](/docs/reference/glossary/#term-layout), where each node sits, is computed every time, never stored, so it can't change what the architecture means. A View says only which way it runs: top to bottom, left to right or the reverse.
