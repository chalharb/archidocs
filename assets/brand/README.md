# Archidocs brand mark

Option C, "Doorway tile": a rounded tile with an arched doorway opening through its base, and an indigo keystone above the arch.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `archidocs-mark-dark.svg` | The mark on light backgrounds |
| `archidocs-mark-light.svg` | The mark on dark backgrounds |
| `archidocs-icon-dark.svg` | App or social icon, dark ground |
| `archidocs-icon-light.svg` | App or social icon, light ground |
| `../../viewer/public/favicon.svg` | The viewer's favicon; follows the browser's colour scheme |

Colours: tile navy `#121A2B` or light `#E6E8EE`, keystone indigo `#5146E5`. The viewer's rail draws the tile in its ink colour, so the mark follows light and dark mode.

Geometry (64-unit grid): the tile is 56 units square with a 13-unit corner radius; the doorway is 20 units wide, springs at y 38 with a 10-unit arch; the keystone runs from 11 units wide at y 12.5 to 7 units at y 21. Keep clear space of a quarter of the tile's width around it.

Beside the mark, set the name "Archidocs" in Overpass 800.
